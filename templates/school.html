These links work like passwords: anyone with them can read your Canvas calendar or announcements. Launchpad keeps them on the Mac and never shows them again here (you'll only see the end of each link). Don't paste them anywhere else. If one leaks, Canvas's Calendar Feed page has a button to reset it.
