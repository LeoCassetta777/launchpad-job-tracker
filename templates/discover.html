🔭 Discover

Postings Launchpad found for you in job-alert emails and on company career pages, scored against your skills profile. Nothing goes into your Pipeline until you press ➕. Checked automatically every morning at 7.

🌐 Web sweep Mon, Wed, Fri at 7:20 AM. The AI web-searches companies' hiring pages (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday: where the jobs on LinkedIn and Indeed are posted) for openings that fit your profile. About 8–10¢ each. For LinkedIn and Indeed themselves, set up their job-alert emails (see Sources).
⚙️ Sources: where Discover looks

📬 Job-alert emails

Sign up for a job board's email alert using your Gmail (the one Launchpad sends reminders from). Launchpad reads only emails from the senders below, read-only, from the last 7 days, and the AI pulls out each listing.

  • 80,000 Hours: on jobs.80000hours.org, set your filters, then “Subscribe to all new job posts” (or to your filtered search). Sender: 80000hours.org
  • LinkedIn: search jobs on LinkedIn, set your filters, turn on “Set alert” (daily or weekly, email). Sender: linkedin.com
  • Indeed: run a search, then “Get new jobs for this search by email”. Sender: indeed.com
  • Others work the same way, like Idealist (idealist.org). Add the sender's address or domain.

🏢 Company career pages

Launchpad reads every open role and the AI keeps the ones that fit your skills profile, so you don't need to name roles. Works for companies whose jobs page is on Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby (look at the address of their “Apply” page: boards.greenhouse.io/…, jobs.lever.co/…, jobs.ashbyhq.com/…). Paste that address, one company per line.

Cost: reading an alert email or scoring a batch is about a tenth of a cent with Haiku, and it counts toward your monthly AI cap.
